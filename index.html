Is my site indexed? A checklist builder

It just turns your domain into the right links, in the right order. What you type stays in your browser.

If a new site has no visitors, the usual reflex is to improve the pages. Better headings, better copy, more links. But a search engine can't rank a page it has never read. The first question is always: has it been indexed at all?

Type your domain below. This builds the exact checks to run, in order, with the right links already filled in. Nothing is sent anywhere; it only assembles links from what you type. The long version, with what to do at each step, is Before you fix your SEO, check that Google can see your site.

Just the domain, with or without https://.

    Reading the result

    Zero results from the site: search is the clearest signal: the engine has not crawled a single page yet. A handful of results on a site with hundreds of pages can mean the same problem, just partial. Confirm in Search Console's Pages report, which counts what is indexed; site: only shows a sample.

    If it's been more than two weeks since you submitted a sitemap and verified the property, and the count is still zero, something is blocking the crawler: a noindex tag left over from staging, a robots.txt disallow, or a password wall. Steps 2 and 3 cover the first two; for the third, open the site in a private window and see if it asks you to log in.
