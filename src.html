    Reading the result

    Zero results from the site: search is the clearest signal: the engine has not crawled a single page yet. A handful of results on a site with hundreds of pages can mean the same problem, just partial. Confirm in Search Console's Pages report, which counts what is indexed; site: only shows a sample.

    If it's been more than two weeks since you submitted a sitemap and verified the property, and the count is still zero, something is blocking the crawler: a noindex tag left over from staging, a robots.txt disallow, or a password wall. Steps 2 and 3 cover the first two; for the third, open the site in a private window and see if it asks you to log in.

    If your site is less than two weeks old, every check above passed and the count is still zero, you probably don't need to fix anything. Google's own guide says crawling can take anywhere from a few days to a few weeks, and asking again for the same URL won't speed it up. Keep the sitemap submitted, don't resubmit it every day, and look again next week. This site went the same way: two days after it launched, Google had indexed 1 of its 11 pages. It knew about 3 more but hadn't added them yet, and 6 it hadn't heard of at all, this tool included.
